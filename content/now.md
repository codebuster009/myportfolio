## May 2026

It's unreasonably hot in the office. The AC near my desk has opinions about whether it wants to be on, and most days the answer is no. Writing this while waiting for a deploy to finish, which is a sentence I've written approximately 800 times in the last three years.

### Work

Day job is shipping AI features at Vantage Cargo. The last few weeks have been about getting a freight chatbot to behave when the user types things like "find me a load near Mumbai for tomorrow, also what's my balance, oh and is Ali free Thursday." Multi-intent prompts are the kind of problem that looks small until you try to ship one.

### Side things

Slowly opening up to freelance work, mostly AI integration for B2B SaaS teams who have a real product and a real roadmap but no LLM person on staff. If that sounds like you, the [services page](/services) has a Say Hi button.

### Reading

Halfway through *Designing Data-Intensive Applications*. Every chapter convinces you that you've been doing your job wrong, but politely. Last week's chapter was on consensus. Now I look at every distributed system with mild suspicion.

### Habits I'm actually keeping

1. Walk before opening the laptop. Twenty minutes, no phone. The day starts differently when the first thing I see isn't Slack.
2. Two evenings a week with no screens after 9pm. I read a real paperback and feel briefly Victorian.

### Habits I'm pretending to keep

1. Hindi practice. The Duolingo streak is almost entirely owl-fear driven.
2. Going to the gym on Wednesdays. The gym hasn't seen me in four Wednesdays.

### Not working on

Anything crypto. Anything web3. Anything that needs me to learn Solidity in 2026. I respect the people doing it. Just not me.
